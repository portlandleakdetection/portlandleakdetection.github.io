What to mention when you call

Helpful details: whether the water meter moves with everything off, any recent bill change (Portland Water Bureau bills every 3 months), where you hear water or see moisture, and whether the home is on a slab. That helps choose between acoustic listening, thermal imaging, tracer gas or a sewer camera.

Useful reading before you book: why is my water bill so high, slab leak detection, emergency leak detection, service areas.
