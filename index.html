Finding leaks without tearing up your home

A hidden leak rarely announces itself with a puddle. More often it shows up as a water bill that climbs for no clear reason, a meter that never quite stops, or a warm patch on the floor. Portland Leak Detection locates the source first, using acoustic listening, thermal imaging, tracer gas and sewer cameras, so repair work can stay small and targeted instead of exploratory.
